10:00About

I like knowing what happens under the hood.

I started at 1337 (42 Network) in Morocco, writing memory allocators, shells, thread-synchronised simulations and network servers from scratch in C and C++, with no external libraries. That’s where I learned how memory, processes and sockets really behave.

From there I founded Cagrex, a developer-assessment platform I built alone end to end, then joined Lysi Consulting as a full-stack developer shipping AI features into a live production platform. I also freelance on Upwork, including a full Next.js build and deployment for a TELUS Digital client. Right now I’m building mobile games with Godot and Unity, with Claude Code in the loop.
